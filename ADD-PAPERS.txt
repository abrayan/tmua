ADDING TMUA PRACTICE

The student works on paper. Each interactive paper is a standalone HTML
file with its questions, maths, images, knowledge steps and player embedded.
Both Normal and Pearson views keep the same untimed learning flow.

BUILD A PAPER FROM THE OFFICIAL QUESTION BANK

  python3 tools/build_bank_paper.py content/tmua-2020-p2-plan.json papers/paper-2/tmua-2020-p2.html

content/official-question-bank.json stores verified questions by source ID.
Each plan group lists originalId and up to three candidate IDs. Select actual
questions from other official years that exercise the relevant knowledge.
Record why each match is useful in the plan's matches array. The builder
checks source IDs and rejects overlap with the paper's original questions.
The player skips followups already used elsewhere during this paper.

Prefer official TMUA questions for followups. If no suitable match exists,
check tmua.co.uk using the user's authorised access. Do not invent a match,
relabel a generated question as official, or silently use a weak substitute.
The current 2020 Paper 2 bank has 20 originals and 48 unique followups.
Question 9 currently has no sufficiently close verified followup. Its full
knowledge steps and solution are present; its candidate list is empty.

The original-source PDFs are preserved as question images, including options
and diagrams. Check that every premise and answer option is fully visible.
Hints, pitfalls and solutions are independently authored and checked against
source answers. Image questions can be enlarged by click or Enter/Space.
They do not yet have full screen-reader transcriptions of mathematical text.

BOOKLET REMINDERS

content/studied-lessons.json maps the seven studied booklets: 84 lessons.
Add one or two hint.recall entries: {lessonId, reminder}. Use canonical IDs,
for example p2-b1-l08. The builder resolves the actual title and PDF page.
Write a short reminder of the relevant knowledge in original wording.
The UI displays Lesson, or Section for the first booklet's METHOD headings.
Do not publish the student's full reference books or personal exam workings.

LEARNING FLOW

1. Initially show only the exercise and answer choices.
2. Record the original first submitted answer once, including a wrong answer.
3. A wrong answer reveals one small knowledge step, a booklet reminder and
   a relevant pitfall. Take it from here returns to the question and hides help.
4. A correct answer always reveals the recap and worked solution.
5. For metadata.practicePolicy = after-miss-up-to-3, an originally missed
   question leads to a verified followup when one is available. After another
   first-answer miss, offer another unused candidate, up to three total.
   Stop after a followup first-answer success or when candidates are exhausted.
   First-time correct originals can advance without a forced followup.
6. Redo never changes recorded first answers. Similar-question first answers
   have their own actual denominator; they are not a predicted TMUA score.
7. Older papers without this policy retain their previous one-followup flow.

Use the familiar coral headings, pale blue working, yellow knowledge and pink
pitfalls. Avoid teacher targets, difficulty labels and generic disclaimers in
student screens. Preserve useful source credits and mathematical warnings.

BUILD A DIRECTLY AUTHORED PAPER

  python3 tools/build_paper.py INPUT.json papers/paper-1/NEW-ID.html

See content/jz-mock-d-p1-preview.json for the data structure. The preview is
 two questions, not a full paper. Use paper-2 for Paper 2. Metadata requires:
format tmua-paper-v1, a unique lowercase id, title, paper (1 or 2), source,
description, accurate questionCount and version 1. Optional practicePolicy
is after-miss-up-to-3. Formulae may use prepared MathML or accessible HTML.

Publish the HTML under papers/. The next automatic build discovers it; no
catalogue edits are needed. Reuse its ID only when correcting the same paper.

PAIRED ROADMAP

assets/roadmap.json lists the suggested pairs in order, each Paper 1 then
Paper 2. Local question PDFs live in assets/bank/. interactiveId links a pair
to a fully authored guided paper when available. A PDF link does not imply
that paper has been converted to the interactive learning flow.

Purchased JZ bundle entries link to verified JZ simulator pages through the
student's account. Paid content is not copied to this public website.
Unavailable pairs stay in comingSoon and do not count as active stages.
Private tutor difficulty estimates and personal performance are kept outside
the published repository. The sequence is a teaching judgement, not a
calibrated difficulty scale or a guarantee of scores.

Manual roadmap marks require an attempt context (first attempt or practised
before) and remain separate from guided-practice scores. A stage completes
only when both marks and review/corrections are recorded. Data stays in this
browser; no account credentials or cross-device tracking are added.

CHECK BEFORE PUBLISHING

  npm test
  python3 tools/test_build_paper.py
  npm run build

Roadmap browser tests additionally run when Playwright is installed or
TMUA_PLAYWRIGHT_PATH points to an existing Playwright package. Inspect actual
questions, answers, recalls, first-correct recap, progressive help, both views,
followup stopping, resume, source-image completeness and narrow layouts.

The build publishes only index.html, assets/, papers/ and the generated
papers/catalog.json. Source content, tests and authoring tools stay out of dist.
